# Archived publication notebooks

This directory preserves historical diagnostics and superseded figure workflows that are not part of the active publication pipeline.

## Contents

- `sa_bess_100hh_negative_underlying_exploration/`: seven notebooks from the 100-household SA-BESS negative-underlying-load investigation. Its legacy `process_pynnlf_output.py` helper is retained under `support/` for provenance.
- `aedp_aggregation_level/`: the one-week AEDP 148-household recovery and checkpoint validation notebook superseded by the full checkpoint workflow.
- `superseded_figures/`: older Section 01-04 visualization and paper-figure notebooks replaced by the canonical numbered build notebooks.

Archived notebooks are retained for historical context. They are not executed by CI, and moved workflows may contain paths that reflect their original locations.

The active paper-facing artifact entrypoints remain the five section-level numbered build notebooks outside this directory.
